# Sprint-Review-and-Retrospective

**How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?**

I first interpret user needs by figuring out what the user wants and what they expect the program to do. After that I can turn those needs into specific features and requirements for the program. User stories help because they explain what the user needs and why they need them and that makes it easier to understand what should actually be developed.


**How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?**

When approaching developing programs, I like the idea of breaking a larger project down into smaller tasks instead of trying to complete everything all at once. In my future development work, I’d like to use Agile processes like Sprint Planning, Daily Scrums, Sprint Reviews, and Sprint Retrospectives. These processes would help me keep organized, communicate with my team, receive feedback, and make changes throughout development when it’s needed.


**What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?**

Being a good team member in software development to me means you should be communicating clearly, completing your responsibilities, and you should be willing to work with and try to support other people that are on your team. I also think it’s important to listen to other people's ideas and feedback and speak up when you need help or when you notice a problem. Everyone on the team may have different responsibilities, but they should still be working together toward the same goal.
